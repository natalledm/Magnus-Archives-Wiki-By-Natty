Found in the Science Fiction and Fantasy shelf of the Oxfam charity shop. Have no title, but Ex Altiora embossed in the spine. Means something like “From Higher” or “Out of the Heights”. 

Written in Latin but had the "From the library of [[Jurgen Leitner]]".

It has several black and white illustrations, woodcuts, showing a mountain or cliffs on an empty night sky. It made who looked feel like falling.

Was burned and apparently destroyed by [[Gerard Keay]].
