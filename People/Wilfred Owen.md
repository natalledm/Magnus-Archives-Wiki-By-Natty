# Wilfred Owen

**Deceased**

Was in the war with [Clarence Berry](Clarence%20Berry.md) and was the only other one who saw [The Piper - War](../Others/The%20Piper%20-%20War.md). Was shot, spent 4 days in a hole and was shell shocked after it.
Wrote a lot and went back to war. Fought like a beast and received a medal for it. Died 2 days later.

Was a real person.

Appears on: [MAG7 - The Piper](../Episodes/MAG7%20-%20The%20Piper.md).
