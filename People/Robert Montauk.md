# Robert Montauk

**Deceased**

Serial killer. Convicted, died in prison a year later, on November 1st [2002](../Calendar/2002.md). Was stabbed 47 times. His cell had a blown out lightbulb.

Killed 40 people between [1990](../Calendar/1990.md) and [1995](../Calendar/1995.md).

His wife used a pendant with a hand with a closed eye on it.
