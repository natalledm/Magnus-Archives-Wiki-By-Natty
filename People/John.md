**Disappeared, probably dead by coffin**

Works for [Breekon and Hope Deliveries](../Places%20and%20Companies/Breekon%20and%20Hope%20Deliveries.md) and was the one to do the contract with [Joshua Gillespie](Joshua%20Gillespie.md) to care for the Do not open coffin in [MAG2 - Do Not Open](../Episodes/MAG2%20-%20Do%20Not%20Open.md).

Said "Delived with gratitude" in a note to Joshua. Was surprised to see Joshua open the door and impressed with the frozen key. Joshua didn't see he leaving the building, so John may be a victim of the [Coffin - Do not open](../Objects/Coffin%20-%20Do%20not%20open.md).
