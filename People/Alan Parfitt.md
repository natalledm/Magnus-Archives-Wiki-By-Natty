# Alan Parfitt

**Disappeared, probably deseased**

Victim of the Doll House person, friend of [Kieran Woodward](Kieran%20Woodward.md). Went to investigate the person and disappeared after. Kieran received a cold metal heart with Alan's name on it and burned it in an incinerator.
Have a brother named Michael that filed his missing report.

Appears on: [MAG5 - Thrown Away](../Episodes/MAG5%20-%20Thrown%20Away.md).
