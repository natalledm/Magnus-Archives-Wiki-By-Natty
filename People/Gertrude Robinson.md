# Gertrude Robinson

**Deceased**

She was the previous Head Archivist on the [Magnus Institute](../Places%20and%20Companies/Magnus%20Institute.md) for over 50 years.

## Appears on:

[MAG1 - Anglerfish](../Episodes/MAG1%20-%20Anglerfish.md) - first mention, where we discover she was the previous Head Archivist of the institute and that she is dead.

[MAG4 - Page Turner](../Episodes/MAG4%20-%20Page%20Turner.md) - we learn she worked there for 50 years.

[MAG8 - Burned Out](../Episodes/MAG8%20-%20Burned%20Out.md) - messy archives complaint.
