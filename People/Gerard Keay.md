Son of [[Mary Keay]], painted the first eye painting we learn, [[The Eye]], on their house. 

He first appear in MAG4, where he buys the book [[Book - Ex Altiora]] from [[Dominic Swain]] and burns it with only this explanation for it:
	“My mother doesn’t always know what’s best for our family.”

We learn he was on trial for the death of his mother but was judged not guilty. No traces of him after it.
