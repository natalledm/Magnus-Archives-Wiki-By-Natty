Was someone who had a collection of strange books.

Scandinavian recluse paying absurd amounts of money for whatever books took his fancy. It was said he’d often have books custom-bound after providing a manuscript, or even commission authors to produce works to his brief.

Disappeared from public by 1995.

Most of all the books from his library have been custom editions of known texts on dæmonology or the arcane, except (for now) the Ex Altiora.
