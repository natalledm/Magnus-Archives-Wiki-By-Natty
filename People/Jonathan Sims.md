
Head Archivist of the [[Magnus Institute]].

Worked at the Institute as a researcher for 4 years before becoming the Head Archivist.

Reader of old files and boss of [[Martin]] and [[Sasha]], he is the one that speaks to us through the tapes.

He doesn't have any patience with [[Martin]] and have some pet peeves against him, due to some discrepancies regarding their work.
