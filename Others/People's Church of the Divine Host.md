# People's Church of the Divine Host

Bizarre cult, they seem to worship the dark or in the dark. Not sure yet.

Their symbol is a **silver hand with a closed eye**, usually worn as a pendant.

40 members were killed by [Robert Montauk](../People/Robert%20Montauk.md), his wife seemed to be part of the cult.
