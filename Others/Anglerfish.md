# Anglerfish

The monster in question looks like an Anglerfish, but uses what resembles a human to lure people instead of a light. It says "Can I have a cigarette?" while the lure appears to be floating only if the victim really pay attention.

Tried to lure in [Nathan Watts](../People/Nathan%20Watts.md), who survived the encounter.

Active during [2005](/Calendar/2005.md) and [2010](/Calendar/2010.md).

Appears on:
[MAG1 - Anglerfish](../Episodes/MAG1%20-%20Anglerfish.md).

Disappeared with:
[John Fellowes](../People/John%20Fellowes.md),
[Jessica McEwen](../People/Jessica%20McEwen.md),
[Sarah Baldwin](../People/Sarah%20Baldwin.md).
