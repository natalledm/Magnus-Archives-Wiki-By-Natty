Very detailed, and at first I almost would have said almost photorealistic, but the more looked at, the more patterns and symmetries formed into a single image, until you'd have difficulty seeing the eye itself.

Written below it were three lines, in fine green calligraphy: 

Grant us the sight that we may not know. 
Grant us the scent that we may not catch. 
Grant us the sound that we may not call.

--
