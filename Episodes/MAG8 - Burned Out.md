# MAG8 - Burned Out

(#0071304)

Statement of Ivo Lensik regarding his experiences during the construction of a house on Hill Top Road, Oxford.

Who speaks in the episode: [Jonathan Sims](../People/Jonathan%20Sims.md)

Statement
Person: [Ivo Lensik](../People/Ivo%20Lensik.md)

Date: March 13th, [2007](../Calendar/2007.md)

Location: [Oxford](../Places%20and%20Companies/Oxford.md), Hill Top Road

He talks about a house in Oxford, Hill Top Road. Ivo works with plumbing and wiring work specifically and was called to help in this house. This house had a very dead and creepy large tree in the backyard. He meets a man dressed in old clothes called [Raymond Fielding](../People/Raymond%20Fielding.md) and he says that he is the owner's house. Raymond showed the deed of the house and land beneath it, so Ivo let him in. The man disappeared and left a scorched patch of wood in his place.

Ivo tells us that his family had a lot of schizophrenia and his dad was obsessed with fractals and its math. It seems his dad was afraid of people with "all their bones in their hands", and was found by Ivo with deep gouges in his wrists and arms, face contorted with fear. Police ruled it as suicide.

After a concussion, Ivo discovered at the hospital that Raymond was real and lived in the 60s.
[Agnes](../People/Agnes.md) is mentioned, a small girl with pigtails that only watched people from the windows of the house. She didn't talk, only when spoken to. People from the house started disappearing, and Raymond said they went away or moved on, until it was only him and Agnes. Raymond disappeared too and Agnes remained. Pets disappeared from the region too. Then small boy called [Henry White](../People/Henry%20White.md) disappeared. They burned the house with her inside but only the skeleton of Raymond was found, missing the right hand.

He went back to work on the house until a day were he felt he was burning up. He was saved by a knock, that was Father [Edwin Burroughs](../People/Edwin%20Burroughs.md), sent by the nurse [Annie](../People/Annie.md). After leaving the priest to bless the house, he found a box with a perfect green apple in the creepy tree. The apple shriveled in his hand and was rotten in seconds, after bursting with tons of spiders and disappearing in a cloud of dust. He destroyed the box that contained it.
