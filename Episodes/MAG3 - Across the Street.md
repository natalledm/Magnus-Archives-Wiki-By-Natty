Statement

Amy came to share the disappearance of [Graham Folger](../People/Graham%20Folger.md), an university colleague. He was enraptured by a strange [Table - strange patterns](../Objects/Table%20-%20strange%20patterns.md) that had snake patterns and a square hole in the middle. The patterns were like optical illusion and made whoever looked at it hipnotized. Amy says it was Graham who restored the table, found in a second hand shop.

We learn that [Not-Graham](../Others/Not-Graham.md) takes Graham Folger life and place itself in it, despise being blonde and Graham being black haired. Also, the voices never matched. Not-G seemed to know all Graham knew and didn't have any trouble with the substitution of Graham.

"Keep watching" was what was written in the notebooks Graham wrote.
