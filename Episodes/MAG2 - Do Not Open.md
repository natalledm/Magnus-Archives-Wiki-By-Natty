Statement of Joshua Gillespie regarding his time in the possession of an apparently empty wooden casket.

Who speaks in the episode: [[Jonathan Sims]]

Statement
Person: [[Joshua Gillespie]]
Date: November 22nd [[1998]]
Location: [[Amsterdam]], [[Bournemouth]]

Here is the first mention of [[John]] of the strange company called [[Breekon and Hope Deliveries]] that deals with strange objects. Joshua receives a coffin to take care of for a year and a half in exchange of 10k pounds.
It seems there was something "alive" inside the coffin, scratched if there was anything on top of the lid and moaned if was raining.
Joshua had to freeze the key to the padlock so he didn't open the coffin while sleeping.

We discover that Joshua was living alone in a whole complex for 2 years.
