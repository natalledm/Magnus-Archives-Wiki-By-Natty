# MAG9 - A Father's Love

(#0020312)

Statement of Julia Montauk regarding the actions and motivations of her father, the serial killer [Robert Montauk](../People/Robert%20Montauk.md).

Who speaks in the episode: [Jonathan Sims](../People/Jonathan%20Sims.md)

---

## Statement

Person: [Julia Montauk](../People/Julia%20Montauk.md)

Date: December 3rd, [2002](../Calendar/2002.md)

Location: [Dartford](../Places%20and%20Companies/Dartford.md), York Road

Julia talks about her father, a serial killer, and tells that he was convicted and killed a year later and only because of his death she can share their story. Her father never defended himself, sat quietly and let people talk for him. Julia makes a small jab at [Magnus Institute](../Places%20and%20Companies/Magnus%20Institute.md) saying that it isn't exactly respected but it is enough for her.
We learn that [Robert Montauk](../People/Robert%20Montauk.md) killed 40 people between [1990](../Calendar/1990.md) and [1995](../Calendar/1995.md). She mentions a book about her father, Ray Cowan’s *No Bodies in the Shed*. She was 12 years old when her father was jailed (so he started when she was 7).
Julia thinks that there was something else happening with her father and that's why she needs to share the story.
The shed her father used came with their house and was only used after her mother disappeared, when things started to get strange. She mentions watching The Witches on the cinema for the first time. During the night, she found her mother's pendant.

> The design had always struck me as beautiful: it was silver, an abstract shape of a hand with a symbol on it that I believe was meant to represent a **closed eye**. ([The Eye](../Others/The%20Eye.md), maybe?)

Julia asked where her mom was to her sleeping dad, who rushed downstairs to open the faucet: sick, salty smell of brackish water flow from it and the light blew out. Her father said her mother was gone and that's it. She believes he didn't kill her because he was devastated. After that, shed time with a new padlock.
Once he came and gently touched her while she faked sleep, smelling blood and brackish water, and said "it will not get you too" while crying. He was a good father during the day and did the killing at night.
She answered a call of her Dad's work friend that identified himself as Detective Rayner ([Joseph Rayner](../People/Joseph%20Rayner.md)?! [Maxwell Rayner](../People/Maxwell%20Rayner.md)?) that made her dad pale. Their conversation made the lightbulb explode.
Apparently her father also took photographs. She saw some, faces of dead people with drawn symbols on them.
2:47, same strange water on tap. All lights die. Monster come to the door, growling rumbling deep with strange tone, and bashes in. She calls the police, run to the shed where there was a blue light. Her father was chanting, with a beating heart and a knife, knelt near a corpse with an open chest. The shed had shelves with jars with hearts inside that pulsed together with the heart in his hand and the light. The corpse had the same pendant her mother had, a silver hand with a closed eye. The monster and darkness all vanished when he pierced the heart.

(So, her father tried to get rid of an entire cult? 40 people that followed the closed eye?)
