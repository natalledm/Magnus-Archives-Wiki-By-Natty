# MAG5 - Thrown Away

(#0092302)

Statement Of Kieran Woodward regarding items recovered from the refuse of 93 Lancaster Road, Walthamstow.

Who speaks in the episode: [Jonathan Sims](../People/Jonathan%20Sims.md)

Statement
Person: [Kieran Woodward](../People/Kieran%20Woodward.md)

Date: February 23rd, [2009](../Calendar/2009.md)

Location: [London](../Places%20and%20Companies/London.md) - Walthamstow, 93 Lancaster Road.

Kieran works as bin man for Waltham Forest Council.
He was part of a small crew: he, David Atayah, Matthew Wilkinson, and [Alan Parfitt](../People/Alan%20Parfitt.md), who – drove – the truck.
The first strange trash near the green bin was hundreds of different dolls heads, all destroyed. So the place where they collected the trash was named Doll House.

After some months, in spring, came the second strange trash. It was a thing and very long piece of paper written in Latin with the Lord's Prayer written over and over. The third bag was worse, with thousand of teeth. They called the police and discovered the house didn't seem connected to the trash.

Alan descent into stalkerish actions and sent a message to Kieran saying "FOUND HIM". After that, he disappeared. Kieran received the last trash bag: a cold metal human hearth with Alan Parfit engraved on it. He asked for a friend to burn it in a medical incinerator.

After statement

[Martin](../People/Martin.md) talked with Kieran and found nothing new.
