Research place where scientists and scholars studies strange cases.

Founded in [1818](../Calendar/1818.md).
Located in [London](London.md), England.

"It is an organization dedicated to academic research into the esoteric and the paranormal."

Anyone that have a bizarre case of the occult, paranormal, monster and other things can come in and make a statement. They will investigate it after.
