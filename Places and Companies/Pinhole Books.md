# Pinhole Books

Bookstore owned by [Mary Keay](../People/Mary%20Keay.md), in [Morden](Morden.md), England.

Used to have lots of contact with [Jurgen Leitner](../People/Jurgen%20Leitner.md) and his library.

Closed in [2008](../Calendar/2008.md) due to Mary Keay murder.

Appears on: [MAG4 - Page Turner](../Episodes/MAG4%20-%20Page%20Turner.md).
